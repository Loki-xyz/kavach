# Kavach — Legal AI Trust Platform
## WashU Law International Vibe Coding Challenge 2026

### The Problem

Every day, lawyers worldwide use AI to draft briefs, research cases, and advise clients. But AI hallucinations — fabricated case citations, leaked privileged information, and ethical violations — create existential risks. A single hallucinated citation led to sanctions against attorneys in New York. In India, where 4 crore cases remain pending and 80% of citizens cannot afford legal representation, lawyers face an impossible choice: work slowly without AI, or work fast and risk their careers.

### Our Solution

Kavach (कवच — Sanskrit for "Shield/Armor") is a legal AI trust platform that makes AI safe, reliable, and defensible for legal practice. It provides four core capabilities:

**1. Citation Verification:** Every case citation is verified against Indian Kanoon and our curated database of 20+ landmark Supreme Court and High Court cases. No more hallucinated citations in briefs.

**2. Privilege Shield:** Automatically detects and redacts seven types of sensitive content: attorney-client privilege, work product, confidential information, personal data, financial data, medical records, and trade secrets. Generates a safe version that can be shared with AI tools without violating ethics rules.

**3. Confidence Scoring:** Every AI output receives a trust score based on citation accuracy, source reliability, and privilege safety. Lawyers know exactly how much to trust AI-generated content.

**4. Case Outcome Prediction:** Predicts win probability, timeline, and remedies for eight common case types based on facts, similar cases, and legal principles.

All operations are logged in an immutable audit trail, providing court-defensible evidence of responsible AI use.

### How We Built It

Kavach was built entirely using AI-assisted development ("vibe coding") with Codex and Nous Portal. The development process demonstrates how lawyers can leverage AI to create sophisticated legal tools without traditional programming backgrounds.

**Tech Stack:** Python FastAPI backend, HTML/Tailwind CSS frontend, Indian Kanoon API integration.

**Development Time:** 2 weeks of iterative development, with AI handling code generation while providing legal domain expertise.

### Impact

Kavach addresses critical needs across the legal ecosystem:

- **For Practitioners:** Safely use AI without risking malpractice claims or ethics violations
- **For Judges:** Ensure AI-generated filings contain verified citations and proper disclosures
- **For Law Firms:** Comply with emerging AI disclosure requirements while maintaining efficiency
- **For Access to Justice:** Enable pro bono lawyers and legal aid organizations to use AI confidently
- **For Legal Tech Companies:** Build trust in AI-powered legal tools

### Responsible AI Use

Kavach acknowledges and addresses key AI risks:

- **Hallucination Prevention:** Citation verification catches fabricated cases before they reach court
- **Privilege Protection:** Automated redaction prevents inadvertent disclosure of protected information
- **Transparency:** Confidence scores show exactly how reliable AI output is
- **Accountability:** Audit trail provides evidence of responsible AI use
- **Human Oversight:** Kavach recommends, never replaces, professional judgment

### Limitations

- Citation database requires regular updates as new cases are decided
- Privilege detection is pattern-based and may miss context-dependent nuances
- Case prediction is probabilistic, not deterministic
- Currently focused on Indian law; expansion to other jurisdictions planned
